Our experience

Over 20 years serving EU projects.

A consulting firm with a passion for learning, development and international collaboration.

Through Erasmus+ projects we build bridges every year between organisations across Europe — from Sweden to Spain, Portugal, Germany, Hungary, Turkey and many more.

Our mission is to give students, teachers and professionals the opportunity to grow — both professionally and personally — through exchanges that inspire new perspectives, knowledge and networks.

  1. 2004

    We successfully carry out a large number of EU projects in Sweden, including Leonardo da Vinci, Comenius, Grundtvig, Central Financial Project and several fund projects.

  2. 2014

    The programmes become part of Erasmus+. We continue running projects with a focus on quality, inclusion and development.

  3. Today

    With our experienced and committed team we handle the entire process — from project idea and application to implementation, follow-up and reporting.

What is Erasmus+

The European Union's flagship programme for education and development.

Since it started in 1987, the programme has given millions of people the opportunity to study, do internships or take part in international projects.

  • Study or do an internship abroad

  • Volunteering projects and international collaboration

  • Networks with schools, universities and businesses

  • Language skills and intercultural understanding

Our work

From the first idea to real change.

An experienced partner throughout the entire Erasmus+ journey.

The four steps that follow are the backbone of every project we run — from idea and funding to implementation, impact and reporting. After that comes what keeps the project in place over time: follow-up and partnership.

  1. 01Strategic compass
  2. 02End-to-end support
  3. 03Impact in focus
  4. 04Compliance
01 Strategic compass

Idea development and the right funding track.

With our strategic compass we help you develop ideas that are relevant, feasible and fully aligned with Erasmus+ priorities. We analyse your needs, shape clear goals and match the project with the right funding track.

We offer

  • We map where your organisation stands today, which needs are strongest and exactly who the participants are. This becomes the foundation everything else in the application rests on.

  • The idea is shaped towards the priorities that apply to this year's call, so the project speaks the same language as the programme from the very beginning.

  • We review which programme strand and application round best matches your ambition, budget and timeline.

  • Needs, goals, activities and results are linked into a chain that holds together. That is the logic assessors look for.

  • We help you find partners whose competence complements your own, and build the relationship before the application is submitted.

02 End-to-end support

Application, budget, consortium building and implementation.

We take responsibility for the entire project process — from application to final report — to ensure quality, structure and efficiency in line with Erasmus+ standards.

We support with

  • We write, structure and review the application against the assessment criteria before it is submitted.

  • The budget is built on the programme's unit costs and flat rates, so that all items are correctly calculated from the start.

  • We put together the consortium, distribute roles and keep communication between partner organisations together.

  • Partner agreements, work distribution and documentation routines are put in place before the project starts.

  • We drive the project forward, keep to the schedule and ensure that reporting is based on documentation collected on an ongoing basis.

  • Risks are identified early and monitored throughout, with support to address anything that deviates from plan.

03 Impact in focus

Dissemination, exploitation and benefits realisation.

We ensure that your results create real and long-lasting value — even after the project ends — in line with EU requirements for dissemination and exploitation of results.

We work with

  • A plan for who should be reached by the results, through which channels and when — not just a closing conference.

  • How the results are taken forward and used after project end, both within your organisation and by others.

  • We follow up on what the project actually delivered and translate that into changes that make a difference in daily work.

  • Experiences are connected to governing documents, competence development and working methods so that they take root.

  • We help you publish and present results where they become searchable and useful for others.

04 Compliance

EU requirements, reporting and audit readiness.

We ensure that your project meets all formal requirements and that you are fully prepared for both reporting and audit.

We offer

  • We check the project against the programme rules and the financial conditions that apply to your contract type.

  • Reports are built on documentation collected along the way, not retrospectively when the details have faded.

  • Participant lists, certificates and receipts are checked so that every line item has the documentation it requires.

  • We go through the material in advance and identify what needs to be completed before the audit arrives.

  • Simple routines that ensure the right thing is documented at the right time, throughout the project.

Project follow-up

Structure, quality and control throughout the project period.

We offer professional project follow-up that ensures quality, goal fulfilment and full compliance within Erasmus+. Through a systematic approach we give you a full overview of the project's development and support you in acting proactively.

Our follow-up support covers

  • We check what has been done against what was planned, so that deviations are visible while they can still be addressed.

  • Actual spend is tracked against budget line by line, with the programme rules as the benchmark.

  • Indicators are tracked during the project, not only when the final report is being written.

  • We check that documentation meets the form and quality the programme requires.

  • Risks are weighed on an ongoing basis and you receive concrete proposals on what should be done and when.

  • Recurring check-ins that keep all parties aligned on the current situation.

  • Before the report is submitted, we go through content and documentation one final time.

Partnership

Build strong European collaborations.

We help you find, evaluate and establish the right partners for your Erasmus+ projects. The focus is on quality, complementary competencies and long-term relationships.

We help you with

  • We identify organisations in programme and partner countries that match the project's focus.

  • The focus is on complementary competence, not on filling slots in the consortium.

  • We make the first contact and keep the dialogue going until the collaboration is in place.

  • Roles, responsibilities and commitments are written down so that everyone knows what they have signed up for.

  • A consortium that holds together over time — and ideally carries through more than one project.

Strong partnerships are the key to high quality and good project results.
